Nando Player Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Nando Player (the "App"), developed by Miguel Ángel Puertas Vélez ("we", "us", or "the developer"), uses when you use it. Nando Player is a music player that works with the audio files stored on your device. It does not require an account and does not operate a server with user profiles or music libraries.

Nando Player is distributed in two variants. The Google Play version uses Google services such as AdMob and Google Play Billing. The Huawei AppGallery version uses Petal Ads and Huawei In-App Purchases (Huawei IAP). Google-exclusive integrations, such as Chromecast and Android Auto, are only available in the corresponding variant.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not ask you to create a Nando Player account and we do not directly request your name, email address, phone number, ID document, or banking details. We have no remote access to your music files or lyrics. Your library, playlists, favorites, recent searches, custom images or GIFs, Tag Editor customizations (title, artist, album, genre, year, track number, album art, and artist avatars), settings, playback history, lyrics parsed from metadata or local files, and listening stats ("Your Summary" and "My Historical Summary") are stored locally and are not sent to our servers. Furthermore, when you voluntarily use Party Mode (Shared Sound) or Chromecast, audio streaming and synchronization take place exclusively within your own local Wi-Fi network or mobile Hotspot, never through Nando Player servers or over the internet.

The advertising and billing providers described in Section 4 may process identifiers, technical information, ad interactions, and purchase data to deliver their services. Nando Player never requests location permission and does not transmit GPS coordinates under any circumstances, including when discovering nearby Party Mode rooms (which relies solely on local network discovery, room codes, or optional NFC).

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The App stores, solely on your phone, the data needed for its features: favorite songs, playlists, recent searches, images or GIFs chosen by you, Tag Editor metadata customizations (title, artist, album, genre, year, track number, custom covers, and assigned avatars), equalizer settings, visual theme, library filters, lyrics extracted locally from your song metadata or local files (such as .lrc files) managed by you, waveform cache, and playback history used to generate "Your Summary" and "My Historical Summary." If you use Running Mode, it also stores session duration, steps, distance, and estimated calories locally.

The App also stores locally in your device preferences: your manual speaker latency fine-tuning offset in Party Mode (-150 ms to +150 ms; in guest mode, streamed audio and cover art are played ephemerally under a no-store policy and are never downloaded or saved as audio files on your storage), your Accessibility menu preferences (mono audio, L/R balance, vocal clarity boost, large controls, full-screen gestures, haptic feedback, voice track announcer, high contrast, extra-large text, and reduced motion), and local App state flags (expiration timestamps for perks voluntarily earned via rewarded ads —4-hour ad-free banner pass, 24-hour theme unlock, and 2-hour Running Mode session—, seasonal visual campaign state, rating prompt state, accepted legal terms version, and—on the Google Play variant where applicable—a minimal technical compliance age-bracket flag and timestamp so ad protections remain active even offline, without storing dates of birth or identity documents). When you choose to share a card from "Your Summary," "My Historical Summary," or Running Mode history, the App temporarily generates a PNG image in local cache to hand it off to Android's system share sheet. Nando Player does not send any of this content to external servers or to advertising or billing providers.

You can delete this information by clearing Nando Player's app data in Android settings or uninstalling the App. Some features also allow clearing their data directly inside the App.

4. Third-Party Services and Local Network Features

Depending on the store from which you installed Nando Player, the App uses the following providers and local network features. Each external provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policies:

4.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

In the Google Play variant, the free version displays ads served by Google AdMob: a banner and, optionally, rewarded ads that you choose to watch. AdMob may process your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, basic App information, and ad interactions to display ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and—where you have consented—personalize ads. Your music, playlists, favorites, and Running Mode sessions are never shared with AdMob.

Where required by applicable law (such as Switzerland, Brazil, and certain U.S. states), the App presents Google's consent form or privacy options (UMP) before or during use of the App, allowing you to choose or update your preferences from the App's settings when available. Furthermore, when Google Play signals that a user is a minor or is in a jurisdiction with mandatory age-signal sharing pending verification, Nando Player automatically applies AdMob age-restricted treatment (AgeRestrictedTreatment), enforces an age-appropriate maximum ad content rating (G or PG as applicable), and disables personalized advertising. You can also limit ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). More information: Google Ads Policy and AdMob Publisher Privacy Policies.

4.2 Google Play Billing (Nando Pro purchase)

In the Google Play variant, if you purchase "Nando Pro," the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your card number or bank account details. The App periodically checks purchase status with Google Play (including local cryptographic signature verification) and stores the entitlement locally to unlock the corresponding features. If Google Play confirms that a purchase was refunded, canceled, or revoked, the App automatically removes Nando Pro access and resets exclusive library filters; temporary offline errors do not revoke the cached status.

4.3 Petal Ads (advertising on Huawei AppGallery)

In the Huawei AppGallery variant, the free version displays banners and rewarded ads provided by Petal Ads. To request, display, personalize where authorized, measure, and protect ads against fraud, Petal Ads may process:

  • device and advertising identifiers, including OAID, and Huawei service identifiers where applicable;
  • operating system version and configuration, device model, and technical hardware characteristics;
  • basic App information, usage, launch/close events, frequency of use, and error logs;
  • network information, IP address, and mobile carrier; and
  • ad interactions such as impressions, playback, clicks, and dismissals.

Petal Ads and its advertising partners may process this information as third parties. Nando Player does not provide Petal Ads with your audio files, custom covers, playlists, favorites, searches, listening history, or Running Mode sessions. The App also does not request location permission and therefore provides no GPS coordinates to Petal Ads.

Before requesting ads, Nando Player presents available consent options so you can choose between personalized and non-personalized ads. You can review or change that choice from the App's privacy settings and limit personalization via your Huawei device's privacy and advertising controls. More information: Petal Ads Privacy and Publishing.

4.4 Huawei In-App Purchases (Nando Pro purchase)

In the Huawei AppGallery variant, the one-time purchase of "Nando Pro" is processed by Huawei IAP. Huawei may process the purchase token, order and purchase identifiers, product, price, currency, country or region, purchase or refund status, and payment method. The App uses Huawei's response to grant and restore access to Nando Pro.

We do not receive or store your card number, banking password, or other bank account details. Nando Player does not operate its own server to store purchase history; it only keeps locally the state needed to enable Nando Pro and can verify it with Huawei IAP. If Huawei IAP confirms that the account no longer holds an active purchase (for example, following a refund or revocation), the App updates and removes Nando Pro access accordingly. More information: Huawei IAP Personal Data Processing.

4.5 Google Fonts (typography)

The App may download certain fonts from Google servers the first time they are needed to render visual themes. Google receives the network request, including your IP address and technical connection details, but Nando Player does not include your music or any other local App content in that request.

4.6 Chromecast (Google Play variant)

If you choose to cast your music to a Chromecast or other Google Cast-compatible device, the App temporarily runs a small local server accessible only within your own Wi-Fi network to stream the file you are playing. This server shuts down when casting ends. The file never passes through Nando Player servers.

4.7 Party Mode — Local Network Shared Audio (Wi-Fi / Hotspot / NFC)

Party Mode (Shared Sound) lets you connect up to 4 devices on your same local Wi-Fi network or mobile Hotspot (1 Host with Nando Pro and up to 3 guest speakers in free read-only mode) to play the Host's current audio track simultaneously.

While a room remains active, participating devices temporarily exchange the following technical information strictly within your local network:

  • the Android device model name (manufacturer and model), local IP address, port, and temporary room code/PIN;
  • local UDP discovery announcements on the Wi-Fi or Hotspot network (host name, room code, guest count, and current track title) to display nearby rooms;
  • a local technical hardware-capability indicator (low-end device flag derived from available RAM and CPU cores) and periodic latency/clock pulses (PING/PONG) to maintain synchronization;
  • basic current-track metadata (title, artist, album, duration, and format), a downsized cover image (up to 256×256 px), and ephemeral streaming of the current audio file protected by a temporary per-track token and a no-store header (without downloading or permanently saving the audio file on the guest phone); and
  • optionally, a local NFC link (nandoplayer://party?...) containing the local IP, port, temporary PIN, and host name if the user enables NFC tag/reader pairing.

No Party Mode files, metadata, or data ever pass through Nando Player servers or are sent over the internet. The Host's temporary HTTP/WebSocket server shuts down and clears its memory as soon as you close the room or the App. Local network privacy notice: because local communication operates directly over your Wi-Fi or Hotspot without TLS encryption in order to prioritize low latency, other devices connected to the same local network could detect the room's UDP discovery announcements; we therefore recommend using Party Mode only on trusted private Wi-Fi networks or your own personal Hotspot.

4.8 In-App Store Updates, System Text-to-Speech (TTS), and Sharing

  • Google Play In-App Updates (Google Play variant): the App uses the official Google Play API to check whether a newer version is available and offer flexible updates; that request is handled directly by Google Play under Google's policies.
  • Google Play Age Signals API (Google Play variant): solely in jurisdictions where required by local law (such as California, Brazil, and other states or countries with minor-protection laws), the App queries Google Play's official API for applicable age features and, where required, the user's age range provided by Google Play (without accessing dates of birth, names, or identity documents), exclusively to comply with the law and apply age-appropriate advertising and consent settings.
  • System Text-to-Speech (TTS): Running Mode voice announcements and Accessibility's Voice Track Announcer pass only the announcement text (elapsed time/distance or track title and artist) to the text-to-speech engine configured on your Android device, which operates under the policies of the provider selected in your phone's settings.
  • Sharing cards: when you choose to share an image from "Your Summary," "My Historical Summary," or Running Mode history, the temporary PNG file is transferred solely to the external app you select via Android's share sheet, subject to that app's privacy policy.
In summary: we do not operate our own servers with user accounts or music libraries. Data leaves your device only when needed for the advertising, billing, font download, store update, or local network features described above. Your music, playlists, favorites, and Running Mode sessions stay on your device or, in the case of Chromecast and Party Mode, exclusively within your local Wi-Fi or Hotspot network.

5. International Data Transfers

Google and Huawei operate global infrastructure. Depending on your country or region, the technical, advertising, and transaction data described in this policy may be processed or stored in a country other than your own. Those providers determine their processing locations and apply the safeguards set out in their policies and agreements. Nando Player does not perform international transfers of your music library or any other data stored exclusively on your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Nando Player is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from minors. To comply with regional app-store minor-protection laws (including, among others, California AB 1043, Alabama HB 161, and Brazil's ECA Digital —Law No. 15,211/2025—), the Google Play variant may receive privacy-preserving age-range signals directly from Google Play via the Play Age Signals API, using them solely to disable personalized advertising or behavioral profiling and apply age-appropriate content limits and consent settings, without storing dates of birth or sharing such signals with unrelated third parties.

7. Security and Retention

Local data remains inside Android's app storage security sandbox until you delete it, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Google and Huawei retain the information they process for the periods defined in their own policies and required by applicable law. In Party Mode, the temporary local server (HTTP/WebSocket) and audio buffers exist only while the room remains open and are cleared immediately when it closes; to protect your local privacy, use this feature only on trusted private Wi-Fi networks or a personal Hotspot. We apply reasonable measures to limit the information used to what is necessary for each feature.

8. Your Choices and Rights

You can control or delete your data in the following ways:

  • clear local data using the options available inside Nando Player, through Android's app settings, or by uninstalling the App;
  • change your ad personalization choice from the App's privacy settings and your device settings;
  • manage purchases, payments, and related data through your Google Play or Huawei AppGallery account; and
  • request information, correction, objection, or deletion of data under our control by emailing the address listed in Section 10.

Because we do not maintain user accounts or our own user server, we normally do not hold data that identifies you. For data controlled directly by Google or Huawei, you should also exercise your rights directly with the relevant provider.

Regional rights: Depending on your country or state of residence (including, among others, the United States, Switzerland, Brazil, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand, and other countries where the App is available), applicable local laws may grant you additional rights to access, correct, delete, port, object to, or withdraw consent for data processing or personalized advertising. You can exercise these choices through the in-App privacy controls (when available), your device and app store settings, or by contacting us at the email address in Section 10.
